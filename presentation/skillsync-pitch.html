Why the name SkillSync?

Connect what students know with the help they need next.

SkillSync describes the app’s purpose: using AI to synchronize a learner’s existing knowledge with personalized support, reinforcing understanding instead of replacing it.

Skill

The knowledge, strategies, and ideas a student has already begun to build.

Sync

Connecting that foundation with the right question, explanation, or review at the right time.

◫
START

Known knowledge

What the student already remembers, understands, or is still working through.

✦
